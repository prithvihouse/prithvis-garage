RIDES & GEAR

Riding is the biggest part of the motorcycle lifestyle. I like the idea of learning new roads while becoming more comfortable on the bike.

Good riding gear matters just as much as the motorcycle. A helmet, jacket, gloves, riding pants, and boots are all part of a responsible setup.

The goal is to ride responsibly, keep learning, and enjoy the process.

RIDING CHECKLIST

  • Wear protective gear
  • Check fuel and controls
  • Plan the route
  • Ride within my skill level
