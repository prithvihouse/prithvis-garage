GARAGE

Welcome to Prithvi's Garage, a personal website about motorcycles, riding, learning, and the experiences that come with owning a sport bike.

My current bike is a black 2026 Yamaha R3. I bought it from Mountain Motorsports in Roswell for about $7,000. Initially, I was planning on getting R7 but as a beginner rider choosing the right bike for right experience is very important.

This garage is about more than a motorcycle. It is a place to document rides, gear, maintenance, and the process of becoming a better rider. I'll also post tutorial on the modification which i'm gonna do throughout the years of my riding.
